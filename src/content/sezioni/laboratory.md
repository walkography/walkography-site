This section holds the files and documentation to build, run and modify the Walkography device. 

**What is the Device?**

The Walkography device is a geolocated instrument that records physiological and environmental data during a walk. The data it collects becomes the basis for constructing the narrative cartographies, the [Atlas](https://www.walkography.eu/atlas/), of the project. 

It has been designed around a few specific commitments:

**•	affordable**

•	**open source**

**•	producing reliable data** (not at medical-grade precision, but stable enough to serve as the basis for narrative analysis)
