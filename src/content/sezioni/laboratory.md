This section holds the files and documentation to build, run and modify the Walkography device. 


**What is the Device?**

The Walkography device is a geolocated instrument that records physiological and environmental data during a walk. The data it collects becomes the basis for constructing the narrative cartographies, the [Atlas](https://www.walkography.eu/atlas/), of the project. 


It has been designed around a few specific commitments:


**•	affordable**


•	**open source and built collaboratively**


**•	producing reliable data** (not at medical-grade precision, but stable enough to serve as the basis for narrative analysis)


The current testing phase, which precedes the release of the first version **WKd_01.01**, has required some compromises. 
These are solutions that appear today as the only way to obtain a stable device, and that may be refined in future versions. For example: the instability of optical heart rate sensors during walking has led me to use an ECG sensor with adhesive electrodes — less comfortable, but considerably more stable.


The first release is being finalised, and will include the firmware code, the wiring schematics, the assembly instructions, and the STL files to 3D-print the device's case.
