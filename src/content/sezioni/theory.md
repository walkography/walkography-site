**Walkography**

Walking is the most ordinary human act, and the least examined.

We treat it as method. To move, observe and perceive through every sense is already to read a territory — and to read it is to begin designing it.

**To walk is to cartograph.**

From that line everything here follows: wellbeing, perception and collaboration are not soft additions to urban design but material for it, gathered one walk at a time.

The method advances by doubt and by drift. To walk is to wander, and to wander is to err — in Latin, one word. We stay errant on purpose: a drift of thought that feeds the drifts of the body.
